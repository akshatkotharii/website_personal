Who I am

Curious by default.

Engineering student, rabbit-hole enthusiast. Pick a thread; there’s more to the story.

I'm Akshat — an engineering student more interested in what you do with knowledge than in having it. Everything I make is a learning curve; that's how I like it.

My sweet spot is AI, data, and everyday problems. If a system can think smarter so a person doesn't have to, I want to build it. That instinct started while tinkering with my father's textile manufacturing systems. I think out loud, ask sideways questions, and work best with people who see things differently.

Beyond the technical, I'm genuinely fascinated by how ideas become companies. I follow the VC and startup ecosystem closely — what accelerators are betting on, which early-stage theses are playing out. I think a lot about what it takes to go from a working prototype to something people actually care about.

Lately, I’ve been also fascinated by the stock market — not just the numbers, but the ideas, businesses, and decisions behind them :)

Outside screens: I play squash, get lost in music, and travel whenever I can — usually to somewhere I've never been. I’m also always up for good coffee and have a soft spot for exploring new tech products and gadgets.
